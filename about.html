Platform

Who we are

Yellow Sapphire Strategic Partners Pty Ltd is an Australian commercial and operating platform based in Sydney. We connect suppliers, buyers, organisations and specialist capability where a trusted relationship and disciplined execution can create mutual value.

Our current focus brings together two complementary capabilities: export and import, where we help structure commercial supply opportunities; and IT project and risk, where we help organisations govern technology and AI-enabled change.

The common thread is not a product category. It is the way we work: understand the real requirement, establish clear boundaries, align the right people, execute responsibly and validate the outcome.
