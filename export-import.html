Process

How an enquiry progresses

A disciplined commercial path to manage expectations and ensure supply readiness.

Step 01

Brief

Tell us the product, specification, quantity, market, required timing and delivery location.

Step 02

Assess

We review sourcing feasibility, supply readiness, documentation needs and commercial fit.

Step 03

Align

We confirm the proposed product, pricing basis, responsibilities, lead time and next decision.

Step 04

Coordinate

Once agreed, we coordinate the supply path and provide visibility through fulfilment.
