Our Services

At Bertrand Export, clients are offered a professional service in a friendly and responsive environment. We make trade between Australia and the Pacific Islands easier with reliable logistics, attentive support, and deep local experience.

Market Access

We connect growers and suppliers with buyers across the Pacific, managing export compliance and local partnerships so your goods reach the right markets.

Logistics Support

From freight coordination to customs documentation, our team handles the transport details to keep shipments moving smoothly from origin to destination.

Quality Assurance

We work with trusted producers and inspection partners to ensure the products we export meet expectations for quality, safety, and consistency.

How do we operate?

Every step is designed to support long-term trade relationships. We focus on clear communication, careful planning, and responsive service for every shipment.

Client Consultation

We begin by understanding your needs, product profile, and target markets to build a tailored export plan.

Shipping Coordination

Our team arranges transport and customs paperwork, ensuring on-time delivery and minimal delays at every stage.

Ongoing Support

We stay in touch after dispatch, managing any issues and helping you grow stronger relationships across the region.

Customs & Compliance

Our export specialists handle customs clearance, paperwork, and regulatory checks to make sure your goods move seamlessly across borders.

We reduce delays and keep your shipments on schedule by managing all documentation and compliance requirements for Pacific trade.

Market Entry Support

We advise on market requirements, product positioning and local partner introductions to help you enter new Pacific markets with confidence.

Our team prepares market briefs, assists with buyer introductions and advises on labelling and packaging standards required by importers.

Logistics Optimization

We optimise freight routes, consolidate shipments and negotiate competitive rates to reduce costs and improve delivery times.

By coordinating with carriers and agents across the region, we create efficient logistics plans tailored to your product and destination.
